Privacy policy

Anima Mundi · last updated November 17, 2026

Anima Mundi is a personal desktop app that runs on its owner's computer. It has no server, no user accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What Google data it uses

  • Permission asked for: "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file). Anima Mundi can reach only the files it created. It cannot see, change, share or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address, read once at sign-in, so the app can show which Google account is connected.
  • The files it creates: it uploads a document or slides you ask it to send, into a folder called "Anima Mundi" in your Drive. When you ask it to work on one of those documents with you, it reads that document's text and applies only the edits you approve.

Where it is kept

The sign-in (the access and renew keys) is stored only on your own computer. Nothing is sent to any server run by Anima Mundi; there is none.

Who else sees it

Anima Mundi never sells, shares or transfers Google user data, except in one case you start yourself: when you ask its assistant to work on one of its documents, that document's text is sent to the AI model provider that runs the assistant (Anthropic's Claude) so it can answer you. It is not used for advertising or to train models by Anima Mundi.

Anima Mundi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove it

Press Disconnect in the app, or remove "Anima Mundi" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either ends its access to your Drive at once. The files it made stay in your Drive until you delete them.
